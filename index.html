Help Center

How do I process a sale?

Navigate to the Terminal tab. Tap on products in the grid to add them to your cart. Use the + and - buttons to adjust quantities. If the customer is a VIP, select their name from the dropdown. Once ready, click Complete Order.

How do VIP points and tiers work?

Customers automatically earn 1 point for every ₱100 they spend. During checkout, they can redeem points as a direct discount (1 point = ₱1). The system tracks their lifetime spending and automatically upgrades them through Bronze (₱5k), Silver (₱10k), Gold (₱50k), and Platinum (₱100k) tiers.

How do I put an item on sale?

Go to the Inventory tab and click 'Edit' on a product. Enter a value in the "Promo Price" field. The system will automatically display a SALE badge on the Terminal and use the discounted price for calculations. If you leave the Promo field empty, it reverts to normal.

How do I backup my store data?

Since Nexus POS operates completely offline, it is crucial to backup your data. Go to the Settings tab and click "Download System Backup". This downloads a single text file containing your entire store's history to your device.

System Info

Why was Nexus POS developed?

Nexus POS was built to empower local microbusinesses with a premium, zero-cost Point of Sale solution. By operating entirely offline within the browser, it eliminates expensive monthly cloud subscription fees and the need for constant internet dependency. Our goal is to ensure that every business—no matter its size or location—has the professional tools it needs to track inventory, reward loyal customers, and thrive.
