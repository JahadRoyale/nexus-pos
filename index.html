Help Center

How do I process a mass order or specific item quantity?

Navigate to the Terminal tab. Tap on a product to add it to the cart. In the cart panel, you will see a quantity box between the - and + buttons. You can click directly into that box and type any custom number (e.g., "50") to instantly process bulk orders without clicking 50 times.

How do VIP points and customizable tiers work?

Customers automatically earn 1 point for every ₱100 they spend. During checkout, they can redeem points as a direct discount (1 point = ₱1). The system tracks their lifetime spending and automatically upgrades them through VIP tiers.

Note: You can completely customize the spending required for Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum by going to the Settings tab and adjusting the VIP Tier Thresholds.

How do I edit a customer's profile or manually adjust points?

Go to the VIP Network tab. Find the customer in the table and click the Edit button. A prompt will allow you to update their Name, Phone, Email, change their VIP Tier manually (overriding the auto-calculator), and manually overwrite their current Points balance. The system will retain their lifetime spend.

How do I put an item on sale?

In the Inventory tab, click 'Edit' on a product. Enter a discounted value in the "Promo Price" field. The system will automatically display a SALE badge on the Terminal, feature it on the Dashboard, and use the discounted price for calculations. Leave the field blank to revert to normal pricing.

How does the Sales Analytics chart work?

On the Dashboard, the Sales Analytics panel pulls data directly from your Order Logs. By clicking Day, Week, or Month, it builds a historical chart comparing the last 7 periods (e.g., the last 7 days) and generates a percentage growth/decline indicator comparing your current period to the immediate previous period, providing accurate real-time business health checks.

The Nexus Mission

Nexus POS was built to empower local microbusinesses with a premium, zero-cost Point of Sale solution. By operating entirely offline within the browser, it eliminates expensive monthly cloud subscription fees and the need for constant internet dependency. Our goal is to ensure that every business has the professional tools it needs to track inventory, reward loyal customers, and thrive.
